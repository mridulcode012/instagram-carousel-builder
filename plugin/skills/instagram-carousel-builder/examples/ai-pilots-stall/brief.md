# Brief: Why AI pilots stall

- **Idea**: most AI pilots impress in a demo and never reach production; the fix is boring, not technical.
- **Pillar**: Founder's Dilemma
- **Audience**: founders and ops leads at 10-200 person companies running their first AI pilot
- **Our angle**: The model was never the problem. Pilots stall on ownership, evals and sign-off.
- **Goal / CTA**: send (to the person who owns the pilot)
- **Variant**: standard

## Evidence
No statistics. The post uses a mechanism (demo vs production), an illustrative effort chart with no
printed figures, and a 4-week plan. No numbers go on a slide.

## Share test
1. **Who sends it to whom?** A founder sends it to whoever owns their AI pilot.
2. **Which slide is the send?** Slide 5, the 4-week plan: it works alone as a screenshot.
3. **What's the "wait, really?"** Sign-off, not the build, is where the time goes.
4. **Why does sending it make the sender look sharp?** It names the real blocker before the pilot stalls.

## Story
PPP: founders already know pilots stall; what they doubt is that it can be fixed, so the room goes to the
better way and the plan (slides 3-5).
