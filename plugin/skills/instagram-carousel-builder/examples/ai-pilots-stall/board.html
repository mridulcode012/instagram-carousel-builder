The design
A printed-paper report. Warm off-white slides alternate with near-black ones, with a single vermilion CTA slide. Each headline is a small italic serif lead-in stacked on one HUGE heavy word, which is the emphasis. Four hairline columns run down every slide, and the visuals are thin ruled diagrams that sit on that grid: square corners, 3px strokes, one element per diagram in vermilion.
