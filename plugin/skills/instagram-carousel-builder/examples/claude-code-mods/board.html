The design
A game screen with brutalist furniture. Near-black slides with pixel-font caps headlines, where one word sits on a tilted acid-green sticker. Every box is outlined in cream with a hard pink offset shadow instead of the arcade theme's stepped frames. Visuals are pixel game UI: hearts, a segmented XP bar, inventory slots, flow chips. The final CTA slide flips to full acid green.
