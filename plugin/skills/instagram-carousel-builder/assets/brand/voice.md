# Buildable Labs: voice and house rules for social slides

Source: *Buildable Labs Brand Foundation*, Version 01, July 2026 (voice). The machine-readable values are in
`brand.json`. The visual design is no longer fixed: every carousel gets its own `design.md`
(`references/design-system.md`). What stays the same on every post is how we sound and the house rules below.

## 1. Who we sound like

An execution-first AI studio that ships outcomes, not proposals. One voice, three registers:

- **Precise.** Technical authority without jargon walls. Every sentence earns its place.
  *"We architect the system, prototype the hard part, and ship on milestones."*
- **Partner-first.** We talk to founders as peers and co-architects, never as a vendor.
  *"We don't wait for a brief. We sit in discovery with you until we both see the architecture."*
- **Conviction-led.** We state a point of view plainly, with confidence but not arrogance.
  *"When AI collapses execution time, judgment is the differentiator. Not headcount."*

**Never:** transactional language, time-based thinking (hours, timesheets), "out of the box",
marketing spin, SOW-only rigidity, "leverage synergies". Lint flags the phrases listed in
`brand.json → voice.never`.

Core lines to echo, not copy: *Anything is buildable, with the right human in place.* ·
*Critical thinking is the moat.* · *Building AI Systems That Ship & Scale.*

## 2. House rules (every design, every slide)

These are the hard gates in the check, so the make workflow writes to them from the start.

- **4-6 slides**, 6 by default; **a real visual on every slide**, slide 1 an infographic.
- **Nothing under 34px** (labels, diagram text and footer included), no weight under 400, text contrast 4.5:1 or better.
- **The headline is the first text on every slide.** No kicker, eyebrow or label above it.
- No overlaps, no clipped text, no dead space.
- **Footer** `BUILDABLELABS.COM` bottom-left (plain text, not a logo), a swipe cue bottom-right on every slide but the last.
- **No logo or brand mark** (ours or anyone's), **no slide or page numbers**, no giant ghost numerals.
- **No self-promotion** and **no engagement bait**; one honest ask, usually a send.
- **Every number traced** to evidence in `brief.md`, with a source line on stat slides.
- No stock photography or photos of people; draw the visuals.
