# pop: notes

Source: ref-8.png: retro-pop Instagram template set (orange/cream/black)

## What was taken
Flat brand-blue slides alternating with off-white, chunky Manrope ExtraBold headlines, one word on a black bar, big flat shapes (corner circle, sunburst rays, wavy rings, bracket lines, thick frame) and a big corner numeral as the counter. Variant `ink` swaps off-white for near-black.

## What could not be matched
- Orange and cream have no brand equivalent: blue and off-white stand in
- Retro display font is replaced by Manrope ExtraBold
- Loud by design: use it for scroll-stopping posts, not for the quiet insight posts

## Rules this theme keeps
Alloy palette only, three brand fonts only, no photography, text/background pairs at 4.5:1 or better.
