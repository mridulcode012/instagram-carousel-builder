# headline-mix: notes

Source: ref-6.png: creator editorial grid ('Asterisk Create')

## What was taken
Two voices in one headline: a small Instrument Serif italic lead-in stacked on one huge Manrope ExtraBold uppercase word (the *emphasis* word), plus a small italic trailing line. Flat colour slides alternating black / off-white, full blue quote slides, quiet footer with handle, 'save for later' and an arrow. Variant `blue` starts on blue.

## What could not be matched
- The huge word must be the *emphasis* word in script.json; without one the headline falls back to a single voice
- Photos and the handwritten asterisk are dropped
- Long emphasis words (over ~10 letters) shrink to fit

## Rules this theme keeps
Alloy palette only, three brand fonts only, no photography, text/background pairs at 4.5:1 or better.
