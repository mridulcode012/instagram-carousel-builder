# cards: notes

Source: ref-1.png (health clinic grid) and ref-10.png (delivery brand grid)

## What was taken
Friendly UI-card system: slides alternate light blue tint / near-black / off-white with full brand-blue fills. Manrope ExtraBold sentence-case headlines top-left; content in big-radius panels (steps as stacked cards with number chip and arrow chip, quote as a chat-bubble thread, stat as a big number in a blue panel). Mono labels in pills, round pill button, small sparkles, dark wave along the bottom of cover and CTA.

## What could not be matched
- Clinic/delivery photography and illustrations are dropped
- Green/teal accents become brand blue and its tints
- Rounded corners (up to 28px) go past the brand's 4px button rule; accepted for this theme as a friendly register, flagged to the designer

## Rules this theme keeps
Alloy palette only, three brand fonts only, no photography, text/background pairs at 4.5:1 or better.
