# glass: notes

Source: ref-5.png: 'Visual / Digital agency' Instagram grid

## What was taken
Photographic dark grid becomes a near-black substrate lit by soft brand-blue light (blue duotone only). Text sits on a frosted-glass card. Wide-tracked uppercase Manrope headline, tiny JetBrains Mono corner meta with arrows, glass disc for the number slide. Quote slides are full brand blue behind glass.

## What could not be matched
- No photography: the light and glass are CSS gradients and blur
- Glass uses backdrop-filter, which renders fine in Chrome but will not carry over to Canva
- Wide letter-spacing makes long headlines wrap early; keep covers to 8 words

## Rules this theme keeps
Alloy palette only, three brand fonts only, no photography, text/background pairs at 4.5:1 or better.
