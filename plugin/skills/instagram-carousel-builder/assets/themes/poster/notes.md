# poster: notes

Source: ref-3.png, ref-4.png: agency social-media poster grids

## What was taken
Orange/yellow poster tiles become brand-blue slides (with a near-black edge vignette) alternating with off-white. Condensed caps headline becomes Manrope ExtraBold caps set tight. The script flourish becomes one Instrument Serif italic word. Ghost word echo, calligraphic swoop line and footer bar are CSS shapes. Variant `ink` swaps blue for near-black.

## What could not be matched
- No condensed display font (Manrope ExtraBold set tight is the nearest)
- Photos of people/products are dropped; the swoop line and ghost word carry the poster feel
- Original orange/yellow accents have no brand equivalent; blue is used

## Rules this theme keeps
Alloy palette only, three brand fonts only, no photography, text/background pairs at 4.5:1 or better.
